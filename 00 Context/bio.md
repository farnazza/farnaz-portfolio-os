# Bio
Research, interface, visuals, motion: I love where they overlap.

Hi, I’m Farnaz, experienced product designer with 7+ years in digital products. I hold an M.A. in integrated design, which taught me to see design as one connected practice rather than a series of handoffs. I move between user research, UX, UI, visual design and motion, and I bring them together so a product feels coherent from the first insight to the last animation.

I've designed for startup and corporate to early stage startups. From discovery phase to shipping the product.

I’m also curious by nature. Lately I’ve been using AI as a playground to test ideas quickly and bring them to life, and I like sharing what I learn along the way.

Beyond client work, I’m launching Herbody Her Mind, a project born from my own personal experience. It offers creative and reflective workshops for women caring for someone with cancer, in a community where they’re seen and supported. It’s still early stage, and it’s where I’m putting everything I know into something I believe in.

I’m open to freelance projects across industries, ambitious startups. Have an idea or a problem to untangle? Say Hello.
