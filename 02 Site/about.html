About me

Hi, I’m Farnaz, a Berlin-based product designer with 7+ years in digital products. My mixed bag of crafts shapes how I think and design.

With an M.A. in Integrated Design, I see design as one connected practice, not a series of handoffs. I’ve worked with corporates and early-stage startups, from discovery to launch. Lately, I use AI as a playground to test ideas fast and share what I learn. I’m also building Her Body Her Mind, creative and reflective workshops for women caring for someone with cancer. I’m open to freelance work and ambitious startups. Have an idea or a problem to untangle? Say hello.
